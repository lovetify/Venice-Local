Help & Tips

  1. Guests can browse listings and deals. Sign in to save favorites, post reviews, and manage your profile.
  2. Use Search, Category, and Sort to narrow results fast. Open Details for photos, map, hours, reviews, and deals.
  3. Tap Save on any business to add it to My Favorites. Saved items are tied to your account.
  4. Business owners can use Owner Dashboard to add businesses, update info, and toggle deals on or off.
  5. If sign in or sign up does not submit, complete the Turnstile check first and try again.
